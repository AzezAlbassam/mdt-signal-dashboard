Verdict

What the ladder needs

The mechanics are sound and the arithmetic is right. Smaller rungs beat bigger ones, patience beats frequency, and bet sizing matters more than target choice. None of that changed.

What changed is where the edge comes from. Blind at-the-money SPY 0DTE has none. One survivor out of twenty-one is below what chance alone delivers.

Selectivity by price action does not work. Every filter that looked good failed out of sample, and the two-filter version was the worst of all. Waiting for confirmation means buying after the move.

You need a source of "which contract". That is the honest gap. The ladder will compound whatever edge you feed it — use the calculator to see what hit rate you would need before committing money to it.
