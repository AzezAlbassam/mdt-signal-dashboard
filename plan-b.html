The correction that changes everything

A +100% trade does not double your account

This is the assumption worth fixing before anything else. Ten trades at +100% only produces $1,024,000 if you bet the entire account on every one of them. Risk a fraction and each win multiplies the account by a much smaller number.

What one +100% trade is worth

Betting everything is the only way ten trades reach a million — and it is also the way you reach zero, since one loss ends the run.

All-in: ten doubles in a row

At a 19.4% hit rate, ten in a row is 1 in 79,459. The plan is not wrong about the destination, only about the vehicle.

Your question, answered

Do two +40% wins equal one +100%?

Almost exactly — 2.1 of them. And that is what makes the smaller rung better, because you hit +40% more than twice as often as +100%.

+40% is the sweet spot. You need 2.1 wins to equal one double, but your hit rate goes from 19.4% to 42.8% — a factor of 2.2. The trade-off very slightly favours the smaller rung, and it compounds over dozens of trades into a large difference in both speed and certainty.

The expiry correction

Short expiries beat long ones

You expected the ladder to want longer DTE. It wants the opposite. Only 0–1 DTE contracts stay profitable in both halves of the record — every longer bucket works in one half and fails in the other.

0–1 DTE calls reach +40% on 72% of trades with a median peak of +88%. They are violent, which is exactly what a ladder needs. Every number on the rest of this page uses them.

Verdict

How to actually run this

Take +25%, not +100%. You were right that two 40s equal a 100 — and because the smaller target is hit far more often, it wins at every goal tested.

Aim at $10,000 first. 90% likely within three years, median twelve months. Then re-base and run the same ladder again. Chasing $1,024,000 in one campaign drops you to 34%.

Use 0–1 DTE calls, not longer ones. This was the assumption most worth correcting: only same-day and next-day expiries survived the holdout. Contracts with 4+ days to run were positive in one half of the record and negative in the other.

Setup supply is the real ceiling. Every timeline here assumes 14 genuine setups a month. Double that and you halve the time; that lever is worth more than any change to the rules.
