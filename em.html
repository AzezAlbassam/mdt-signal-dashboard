The expected move, rebuilt

Two bands per horizon. One prices the expiry itself and reacts to events, the other reads a smooth volatility curve and does not. The gap between them is the zone.

What this is. An independent reconstruction, built from a public option chain. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TrendSpider or any YouTube channel. Two constants in the model are still unresolved and are shown as a fork rather than quietly chosen. These are levels, not advice, not a signal and not a forecast.

How each band is built

Every band is a close, a volatility and a horizon, plotted symmetrically about that close. The horizon is counted in real trading sessions, so a holiday shortens it.

Why two families. The plain family takes the at-the-money straddle of the actual expiry, so an inflation print or a Fed meeting widens it immediately. The iVol family reads a thirty-day constant-maturity volatility, which barely moves on the same day. Across the ten observations below, the plain daily band ran from 0.59 to 1.04 of VIX while the iVol daily band held between 0.88 and 0.96. Nothing derived from a volatility index can produce that first range.

How a session is scored

Published before any data exists, so that a later change to these rules is visible as a change to the rules.

Against a level
Untouched price never reached it.
Tagged price reached it and the close came back inside.
Broken the close finished beyond it.
Touching to the cent counts as reaching.
Against a zone
Untouched price never entered.
Entered price traded in and stopped inside.
Crossed price went through the far edge.
Closing beyond is recorded separately.

A band is only ever scored against a session strictly after the close it hangs on. The code refuses the alternative rather than trusting the caller.

Accuracy

Two separate questions, never added together. Reproduction fidelity asks whether the arithmetic matches the chart. Calibration asks whether the bands contain the market at the rate their own maths implies. A band can be reproduced perfectly and still be badly calibrated.

The two open constants

Both are single flags in the model. The nightly record stores every candidate whether or not it is the published one, so the archive can settle each without fetching anything again.

Research only, not financial advice. Band levels describe what the options market is pricing, not what will happen. Options can lose the entire amount invested. Price and chain data are delayed.
