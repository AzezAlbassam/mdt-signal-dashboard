    Read H1's small p correctly. The §4 null swaps projections between symbols, so the
    p-value asks only whether a symbol's OWN projection fits it better than another
    symbol's would. It does. That is not the hypothesis: H1 asks whether HCP beats a
    no-change line, and the effect is negative, so H1 fails on its first condition.
    HCP carries symbol-specific information and is still far worse than doing nothing.
