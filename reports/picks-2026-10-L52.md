## How the five were chosen (rule fixed before the ratings came back)

1. **Signal** — the only long-hold rule that beat random entries in this repo: weekly
   RSI(14) crosses back above 30, buy at the next week's open, hold 52 weeks (study 5,
   `reports/study5-confirm.txt`: 539 trades on unseen US stocks, win 75.9% vs 65.3% for
   random entries, median +19%; edge only +2.6% before 2018). Nothing tested here
   identifies stocks "ready to explode".
2. **Universe** — TradingView screener, US-listed common stocks ≥ $2B (3,282), sorted by
   weekly RSI. Restricted to ≥ ~$45B because study 5 was tested on that universe (stage-1
   large caps); smaller names would be untested. HONA excluded (2026 spin-off, too little
   weekly history).
3. **Candidates** at or near the trigger (weekly RSI, last completed week 2026-09-28 →
   intraweek 2026-10-08; checked against `engine/swing.js` + `engine/indicators.js` on
   stored bars, same values to 0.1):
   PEP 29.9 → 33.6 · AON 27.8 → 31.7 · CRH 29.2 → 29.1 · O 29.4 → 29.2 · MCD 23.2 → 27.7 ·
   NKE 26.3 → 29.4.
4. **Rating** — each rated by the ticker-analyst pipeline (one agent per ticker, current
   filings and news via search, reverse DCF). The skill's rubric file was missing, so nine
   pillars with stated weights were used: valuation 20 · moat 15 · thesis survival 15 ·
   financial health 10 · value capture 10 · growth 10 · value creation 8 · capital
   allocation 7 · clarity 5. A FATAL kill hypothesis caps at 5.4. **Top 5 by score.**
