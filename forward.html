Why this page exists

You can settle a big claim in five trades

The number of forward calls needed to prove an edge scales with 1 ÷ (edge)². That has a blunt consequence: the bigger the claim, the less evidence it takes to confirm — and the longer a big claim goes unconfirmed, the more that silence means.

The asymmetry. Establishing a 90% claim takes five calls made in advance. Across 98 public posts spanning four years, there were two. That is not proof of anything about him — but it means the claim has never actually been tested, by him or by anyone. Five honest calls would do it. This page is for making them.
